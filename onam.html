    The four events

    Four ways to spend the morning.

    Two run before the break, two after. Every event has its own registration form — pick the one you want and take your team in.

    Who can participate

    If you are in IT and in 2nd or 3rd year, you are in.

    TECHAVAM is an internal department celebration, so entry is limited. Read this before you fill a form — it saves a rejected registration later.

    Eligibility

    IT department only

    Open to Information Technology students in 2nd year and 3rd year. No other department or year is eligible, so the form does not ask for a department.

    Teams

    Four members, maximum

    Enter alone or with up to three others. Members 2, 3 and 4 are optional on every form — a team of one is a valid team.

    Registration

    One form per event

    Each event has its own Google Form and its own QR code. Both Slot 1 events run at the same time, and so do both Slot 2 events — so pick one from each slot at most.
